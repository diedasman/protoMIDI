# protoMIDI

>ProtoMIDI is a custom macropad in a MIDI controller style. Utilizing PB86 switches and a rotary encoder, it offers a tactile andresponsive experience for musicians and producers. The macropad is designed to be compact and portable, making it ideal for on-the-go music production. The OLED display provides visual feedback. Installed with ZMK firmware, protoMIDI can be configured for various applications, including controlling digital audio workstations (DAWs), triggering samples, and adjusting effects in real-time. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned musician, protoMIDI is a powerful tool to enhance your music production workflow.

>It is build entirely out of off the shelf components, making it easy to assemble and customize. The macropad features a FR4 protoboard with 3D printed case and metal mounting hardware, ensuring durability. The PB86 switches offer a satisfying tactile response, while the rotary encoder allows for precise control over parameters. With its compact design and versatile functionality, protoMIDI is a must-have for any musician looking to elevate their music production setup.

> Refer to the resources folder for the 3D printed case files, PCB design, and component configuration. The assembly process is straightforward.
